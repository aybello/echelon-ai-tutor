# WPI Wastewater Formula Source Notes

**Scope:** Corrections to the WPI Class II and Class III Wastewater formula sheets on September 28, 2026.

## Governing rule

The sheets teach calculations. They do not establish operating setpoints, compliance limits, chemical doses, land-application rates, or reporting requirements. Learners must use the current facility approval or permit, validated procedure, sampling plan, and applicable jurisdictional rules.

## Ontario sewage works

Ontario states that sewage works must comply with the specific terms and conditions in their Environmental Compliance Approval. Its Design Guidelines explain that effluent requirements are receiving-water based, may be more stringent than a normal treatment level, and become approval conditions.

- Environmental Compliance Approval: https://www.ontario.ca/page/environmental-compliance-approval
- Design Guidelines for Sewage Works, effluent quality requirements: https://www.ontario.ca/document/design-guidelines-sewage-works/design-considerations-sewage-treatment-plants

## Canadian acute lethality

For systems subject to the federal Wastewater Systems Effluent Regulations, acute lethality means undiluted effluent causes more than 50% mortality in rainbow trout during a 96-hour period. The current test and response requirements depend on the system and regulation. A toxic-unit screening calculation does not replace the governing accredited-laboratory test.

- Environment and Climate Change Canada, Acute lethality: https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/wastewater/system-effluent-regulations-reporting/overview/acute-lethality.html
- Wastewater Systems Effluent Regulations, SOR/2012-139: https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-139/FullText.html

## Corrections made

- Removed fixed Ontario ECA BOD/TSS wording, universal wastewater CT ranges, generic UV reuse targets, and unsourced biosolids application values from the Class II sheet.
- Corrected Class II ozone-dose unit presentation. `g/m³` is numerically equal to `mg/L`, so the former extra `× 1,000` in the displayed formula was removed.
- Corrected Class III denitrification and F/M worked calculations.
- Removed the unsafe generic industrial-user limit formula and lead example.
- Reframed biosolids, toxicity, greenhouse-gas, and metal-loading material as calculation or reporting frameworks that require the governing local method and approval.
