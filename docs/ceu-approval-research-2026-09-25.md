# Ontario CEU Approval Research

**Date:** 2026-09-25  
**Purpose:** Confirm what an Ontario operator CEU course can be and distinguish public pilot access from approved-credit status.

## Findings

A CEU course can be a focused topic course. Ontario's Director-approved guide lists directly relevant course subjects such as drinking-water treatment and distribution, sampling and laboratory techniques, disinfection, SCADA and process control, water storage, water quality, applied mathematics, hydraulics, maintenance and emergency/risk management.

For a drinking-water course to become **Director approved**, the Ontario guide requires structured learning with two-way learner-provider contact, documented learning objectives, a way to verify learning, a course evaluation, qualified design and delivery, and a compliant completion certificate. The guide includes e-learning and other online learning within distance learning when the criteria are met.

Wastewater-only courses cannot receive Director approval for drinking-water certificate renewal. They can be submitted to OWWCO for a review of course length or CEU value. OWWCO defines one CEU as ten hours of approved training.

## Echelon current position

Echelon's current ten-course catalogue is public and open for inspection. A visitor can open every course and read its lesson slides without an account. Sign-in is only required to save progress, take the final exam and obtain the non-credit pilot learning record.

The current courses remain non-credit pilots. They must not claim Director approval, approved CEUs, accreditation, qualification or regulatory recognition until the applicable course review has been completed and written approval is received.
