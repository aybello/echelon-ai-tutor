# OIT Wastewater Source-Repair Batch — 2026-08-30

## Scope and learner-content constraints

This is a controlled answer-length remediation batch for **OIT Wastewater**. The initial read-only audit identified 241 length-biased questions from 1,033 learner-visible items. The present candidate set is limited to the first 25 audited items. No stem, answer key, explanation, or distractor can be published unless the candidate passes structural validation, independent external review, and `in_review` staging.

## Drafting safeguards

- Preserve a single defensible answer and distinguish operational principles from legal obligations.
- Never make a distractor read as a recommended unsafe act, a delayed spill report, a bypass of lockout, or a failure to respond to an alarm.
- Remove unsupported precise performance ranges, named organisms, or regulatory claims when they are not required to teach the tested concept.
- For Class A/Class B biosolids wording, make the United States regulatory context explicit or hold the item for Ontario-specific source review.
