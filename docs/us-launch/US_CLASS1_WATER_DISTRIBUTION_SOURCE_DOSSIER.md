## Calculation boundaries

Use U.S. customary units as Echelon’s course format while disclosing that WPI standardized calculations are presented in both U.S. Standard and metric units. Approved calculation families are:

- main, tank, and reservoir volume;
- flow rate, velocity, pressure/head conversion, and basic hydraulic relationships;
- basic dose, feed rate, pump-setting, CT, blending, and mass concepts only where a corresponding Class I task supports their use; and
- unit conversions directly supported by the WPI formula table.

Every calculation must show its units, formula, substitution, result, and reasonableness check. Do not use a formula solely because it appears on the WPI table; the criteria must support the learning objective.

## Mandatory exclusions and claim controls

Never state or imply that the course:

- is WPI, ABC, EPA, state, provincial, Tribal, or regulator approved, endorsed, accredited, adopted, or affiliated;
- establishes eligibility, satisfies an application, experience, training-hour, renewal, or licence requirement; or
- guarantees a score, certification, licence, facility authorization, or scheduling result.

Do not present state pressure-loss thresholds, required notice/sampling procedures, return-to-service requirements, discharge requirements, local cross-connection rules, water-loss reporting, or operational SOPs as universal answers. EPA’s loss-of-pressure page used in this dossier contains jurisdiction-specific Wyoming/Tribal requirements and may be used only to teach the technical hazard and response reasoning—not a nationwide numeric rule.

Exclude higher Classes II–IV, specialty examinations, very-small-water-system pathways, backflow-prevention assembly-tester credentials, actual WPI questions, and copied/reconstructed content.

## Research gaps to preserve

- WPI publishes area weights, tasks, cognitive counts, and calculation counts; it does not publish item-level formula allocation, answer rationales, a pass score, or a complete task hierarchy.
- The formula table is published for standardized distribution exams generally, not as a Class I-only formula map.
- The WPI page calls the criteria 2025 but the current linked PDF uses a 2026 upload path; retain the criteria label and recheck immediately before authoring/release.
- State credential adoption and implementation vary. State rules determine eligibility, licence, application, renewal, reporting, and facility requirements.
