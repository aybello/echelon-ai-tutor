# Phase I U.S. Launch Recommendation — Official Verification

**Decision:** Proceed with a tightly scoped Phase I launch of **shared WPI Class I study material** in all **20 verified state/stream routes** below. This is an exam-preparation routing decision only; it does **not** establish certification eligibility, provider approval, or facility authorization. Official-source check date: **2026-10-10**.

### Routes held

**None.** The supplied verification designates all four shared streams as safe in Alabama, Alaska, Arizona, Georgia, and South Carolina. The release is conditional on the route labels and exclusions in this report; it is not a blanket state, grade, level, facility-type, or credential claim.

## Required route-specific positioning

- **Alabama:** Never call the water-treatment route “Alabama Grade I”; the verified mapping is WPI Class I to **Alabama Grade II**. Keep wastewater-treatment preparation to **lagoons** and wastewater collection to **public** systems. Do not position shared material for advanced groundwater, surface-water, higher-process wastewater, industrial/other non-municipal treatment, or unverified small/industrial collection variants.
- **Alaska:** Use the state’s **Provisional/1** or **Level 1** terminology; DEC still calls the standardized tests “ABC exams,” while identifying ABC as WPI. Exclude Alaska’s separate small untreated/treated water, wastewater stabilization pond, and Levels 2–4/system-complexity offerings.
- **Arizona:** Use **Grade 1** only, not universal facility authorization. Exclude facilities listed as exempt in R18-5-102 and Grade 2–4/higher-complexity operations; wastewater Grade 1 has the stated local facility limits, including that aerated lagoons are Grade 2.
- **Georgia:** Water-treatment and biological-wastewater Class I are advanced tiers with Class II and experience prerequisites. Do not call the unnumbered distribution or collection credentials “Georgia Class I” licenses; describe them as preparation for the **Georgia Board-linked ABC/WPI Class I exam**. Exclude Classes II–IV/IIIG, Class IV pond-course preparation, industrial wastewater, laboratory, very-small-water, and small-wastewater offerings.
- **South Carolina:** Use **D Level**, not an assertion that Class I is entry level; Water Treatment begins at Level E. Keep wastewater treatment to the **biological** track, excluding Physical/Chemical (Industrial Waste) Wastewater. Describe collection as the **voluntary** WEASC VCC credential, not a state Board license. Exclude Level E, C–A/Classes II–IV, and unverified specialty exams.

## Common nonclaims and exclusions

Every Phase I product page, ad, checkout disclosure, and support script must make clear that the product:

1. is **study preparation only** and is not approved, accredited, endorsed, affiliated with, or a substitute for a state agency, Board, DEC, ADEQ, ADEM, WEASC, WPI, or their rules;
2. does **not** guarantee a passing score, eligibility, scheduling approval, certification, licensure, facility authorization, renewal, or upgrade;
3. does **not** replace official applications, fees, education, required courses, experience, prerequisite certificates, Board/agency approval, documentation, continuing education, or state-specific system/facility rules; and
4. covers only the verified **Class I/Grade 1/Provisional-1/D-level** route named above, not higher levels/grades, specialty streams, industrial/physical-chemical tracks, laboratory, maintenance, biosolids, backflow, or unverified small-system/lagoon formats.

**State-required certification gates remain controlling:** Alabama requires application, at least 70%, and applicable experience; Alaska requires application, a $100 review fee, and education/experience rules; Arizona requires the applicable exam, at least 70%, Grade 1 education minimum, and ADEQ result certification; Georgia requires Board approval and its education/course/experience/prerequisite requirements before credentialing; South Carolina requires Board approval and applicable experience/promotion rules, while VCC has separate employment/experience, application, renewal, and continuing-education requirements.

## Next gated work before selling

1. **Curriculum-to-route gate:** Confirm that each module, practice item, title, and learning outcome is confined to the applicable shared WPI Class I content. Do not release material as state-specific preparation until that internal mapping is documented; the supplied findings verify exam alignment, not the quality or completeness of any study product.
2. **Claims-and-label gate:** Create state/stream-specific product metadata and reviewed copy using the exact labels in the release matrix. Implement the Alabama Grade II, Alaska Provisional/1, Georgia unnumbered distribution/collection, and South Carolina D-level/voluntary-VCC distinctions as hard publishing rules.
3. **Exclusion-and-checkout gate:** Publish the common nonclaims plus the route-specific exclusions above in the purchase flow and support materials. Prevent cross-selling from these routes into excluded grades, classes, facility types, specialty exams, or South Carolina industrial/physical-chemical wastewater.
4. **Eligibility-referral gate:** Provide an official-state next-step link or referral for applications, prerequisites, experience, fees, approval, and certification decisions; do not have sales/support make eligibility determinations.
5. **Final source recheck gate:** Immediately before release, recheck the cited official pages and WPI criteria for current exam labels, routes, and rule changes, then record the release-date review.
