## Calculation boundaries

Use U.S. customary units as Echelon’s course format while disclosing that WPI standardized calculations are presented in both U.S. Standard and metric units. Approved calculation families are:

- flow, volume, area, tank geometry, velocity, detention time, head/pressure/force;
- chemical dose, active strength/purity, daily chemical use, feed rate, feed-pump setting, dilution, blending, and batch solution;
- basic CT as residual concentration × contact time, framed as a technical concept—not an operator’s facility-specific compliance determination;
- filter loading/hydraulic or surface-overflow rate and basic backwash/filter-performance interpretation; and
- basic static-versus-pumping well-level interpretation.

Every calculation must show its units, formula, substitution, result, and reasonableness check. Do not invent an unpublished task-to-formula allocation.
