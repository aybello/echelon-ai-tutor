# Mississippi water-distribution readiness dossier

**Route ID:** `ms-water-distribution`
**Existing matrix label:** state-specific
**Research status:** official-source dossier only; no questions, options, explanations, bank data, or release created.
**Decision:** **Narrow**

## Exact state and stream

**Mississippi (MS) — water distribution.** The state-specific credential boundary supported by the current official rule is the **Mississippi Bureau of Public Water Supply Class E waterworks operator certification** when the operator's only job responsibility is operation and maintenance of distribution system(s). The rule defines a distribution system as water mains, repumping facilities, and appurtenances past treatment. [1]

## Credential and examination boundary

The credential is not a generic or multi-state distribution credential. It is the Mississippi Department of Health (MSDH) Bureau of Public Water Supply waterworks-operator certification at **Class E**. Class E applies to water systems that purchase water only and provide no additional treatment, and also to an operator whose only responsibility is operating and maintaining distribution system(s). [1]

Class E applicants must meet the stated education and experience prerequisites and pass the Bureau-required written examination. The controlling rule further states that the Bureau prepares written examinations to assess operator knowledge, ability, and judgment; eligibility to sit requires attendance at a Bureau-sponsored waterworks-operator short course within the preceding 12 months. [1] MSDH currently provides an initial, retake, and upgrade request path for a computer-based public-water examination, and its currently linked candidate bulletin describes MSDH Bureau of Public Water Supply drinking-water certification examinations administered by PSI. [2] [3] [4]

**Boundary conclusion:** the official sources establish a Class E, distribution-only certification level and an associated Bureau-required written examination. They do **not** publish the Class E test title, a Class E examination content outline, task inventory, question count, domain weights, or passing standard. The public candidate bulletin is an administration guide rather than an exam blueprint. Therefore, the only defensible authoring boundary is a small set of Class E distribution operational duties directly stated in current official materials; it is not a basis for representing coverage of the complete Class E examination.

## Source freshness and conflicts

The primary certification rule is currently available through the official Mississippi Secretary of State administrative-code repository and is the controlling source for the Class E credential and examination requirement. The live MSDH program page, last reviewed on March 13, 2025, links to both the public exam handbook and the operations manual. This supports treating the materials as the currently supplied official program materials on the retrieval date. [1] [2]

The linked candidate bulletin is dated March 2021 and the operations manual is dated May 2019. Neither is a current, class-specific examination blueprint. The dated manual may support only the narrowly stated operational-duty objectives below, and it must be rechecked against a newer MSDH operations manual if one is published. The bulletin cannot support content selection beyond examination administration because it does not identify a Class E content domain or distribution test specification. [4] [5]

The assignment manifest’s former MSDH handbook link at `https://msdh.ms.gov/page/resources/20537.pdf` was unavailable at retrieval. MSDH’s current certification page instead links to the March 2021 bulletin at source [4]. The unavailable manifest link was not used as evidence. No conflict was found between the controlling rule and the currently linked materials on the Class E distribution-only classification; the material limitation is the absence of a published Class E exam blueprint.

## Learner-level scope and exclusions

**Learner level:** prospective or renewing Mississippi Class E waterworks operators whose supported boundary is distribution-only operation and maintenance, not treatment-plant operation.

**Supported scope:** the exact Class E classification, distribution-system boundary, certification/examination eligibility context, and the limited Class E operational duties expressly described in the official operations manual. The proposed scope is operational and regulatory-awareness material only. It does not claim to mirror the full MSDH examination.

**Excluded:**

- Class A, B, C, or D waterworks-operator certification, including treatment-process content.
- Treatment design, water-source operation, chemical dosing, process control, engineering design, construction specifications, or facility-specific procedures.
- Backflow tester licensing or cross-connection tester examinations.
- Any numerical threshold, sampling frequency, compliance rule, reporting duty, or emergency procedure not expressly within the stated Class E official-source boundary.
- Assertions about the number, weighting, passing score, domains, or tested tasks of a Class E examination, because MSDH has not published an official Class E examination outline in the reviewed materials.
- National, WPI, other-state, commercial-preparation, blog, or model-derived content.

## High-level objectives for a future narrow dossier

These are source-bound, high-level objectives only; they are not questions or candidate content.

1. Identify the Mississippi Class E classification as the credential boundary for an operator whose sole responsibility is operation and maintenance of distribution system(s), and distinguish it from treatment-oriented Classes A through D. [1]
2. Recognize the Class E distribution-system boundary stated by the rule: water mains, repumping facilities, and appurtenances past treatment. [1]
3. Recognize the official Class E operational-record and routine monitoring/inspection duties stated in the MSDH operations manual. [5]
4. Recognize the manual’s Class E distribution residual-check and wholesaler-notification condition, without extrapolating to unsupported corrective procedures or broader examination coverage. [5]
5. Recognize that Class E certification requires meeting the Bureau’s qualification requirements and passing the required written examination after the stated Bureau-sponsored short-course eligibility condition. [1]

## Readiness decision

**Narrow.** Mississippi has an official, specific distribution-only credential boundary: Class E waterworks operator. The rule also requires the appropriate written examination, and the public MSDH materials identify an active computer-based examination program. That is enough to preserve a limited future Class E distribution dossier.

It is not **Suitable** for a broader or exam-representative candidate package. The available official materials do not publish a Class E exam title, blueprint, content outline, domain weighting, test length, or task list. The only direct content support is the dated but currently linked Class E operations-manual section. Under the readiness contract, only a **Suitable** dossier may generate review-only JSON candidate batches. Accordingly, this Narrow decision does not authorize authoring, staging, or generating any candidate package.

## Bank recommendation

**No unique bank is recommended or proposed at this time.** Although a unique Mississippi Class E distribution credential boundary exists, the evidence does not support defining a bank as representative of a named Class E examination. A future bank-identity decision requires a current official MSDH Class E examination name and content outline, or written official confirmation of the class-specific examination scope. Before any later proposal, recover and reconcile any prior U.S. Class I package as required by the readiness contract; do not duplicate or overwrite it. [6]
