# Florida Water Distribution — State-Specific Readiness Dossier

**Decision:** **Narrow**

## Credential and examination boundary

This dossier is limited to the **Florida Department of Environmental Protection (DEP) Water Distribution System Operator Level 3 examination**. It is not a dossier for Florida water-treatment, wastewater, collection-system, general public-water-system, or another state’s credential.

The examination is a distinct DEP distribution-operator examination at Level 3. DEP’s current licensing page states that individuals newly seeking a water-distribution license must begin at Level 3. The official distribution examination outline identifies four levels and states that each level examination is two hours, contains 50 multiple-choice items, and has its own stated subject-area list. The Level 3 list is therefore the only examination-content boundary used here. [1] [2]

This is an **examination-preparation boundary, not a full licensure boundary**. An applicant must have a high-school diploma or equivalent and complete a Department-approved Level 3 training course within five years before the examination to be eligible to sit for it. Passing the examination alone does not grant the license: Florida’s current rule requires documented Level 3 distribution experience of 2,080 hours for licensure, and DEP explains that examination may occur before the mandatory experience is obtained. [3] [4] [5]

Level 1, Level 2, and Level 4 are excluded. Levels 1 and 2 have progression prerequisites, and DEP’s published pathway directs new distribution applicants to begin at Level 3. The retained Level 4 material is not combined with the Level 3 route. [2] [3] [5]

## Readiness finding

**Narrow** is appropriate. Florida has a current, regulator-issued Level 3 examination boundary; a current official program handbook; current official program pages; and a regulator-issued Level 3 subject-area outline. This supports a limited, future candidate dossier for the stated Level 3 examination only. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]

The route is not Suitable for a broad or level-combined bank. The public Level 3 outline provides subject headings but no published weights, task statements, subtopic detail, formula-to-objective mapping, or level-specific competency depth beyond the headings. It says an examination item *may* come from the listed areas. Those limitations prohibit inferring a comprehensive blueprint or detailed technical, regulatory, calculation, safety, or site-operating content from the headings alone. [1]

## Learner level and permitted scope

The intended learner is an applicant for the **Florida DEP Water Distribution System Operator Level 3 examination**, who has completed or is completing the required Department-approved Level 3 course and is pursuing the entry point in Florida’s current distribution-licensing path. This designation does not represent authorization to operate a system independently, certify compliance, make engineering decisions, or receive a license. [2] [3] [4] [5]

### Exclusions

The following are outside this route:

- DEP Water Distribution System Operator Levels 1, 2, and 4, including their eligibility and licensing pathways.
- Water-treatment-plant, wastewater-treatment-plant, wastewater-collection, and non-Florida operator programs.
- Detailed statutory, permit, monitoring, notification, reporting, or customer-notice requirements not supported by an additional current official source.
- Site-specific standard operating procedures, emergency response, field direction, construction or repair methods, engineering design, or judgment about a particular distribution system.
- Any assertion that examination success independently establishes the work experience or other conditions for licensure.

DEP’s operator-duty rule confirms that water-distribution operators have operational, maintenance, logging, notification, and reporting responsibilities. That rule is not sufficiently granular, by itself, to support future detailed operating or compliance content under this Narrow route. [6]

## Source freshness and conflicts

The principal program handbook is revised June 2026, making it the newest substantive program document located. It aligns with the February 2026 DEP examination page on eligibility and the distinction between examination eligibility and eventual license issuance. [3] [4]

The controlling licensure rule, Rule 62-602.300, was last amended effective November 6, 2025. The current rule confirms separate distribution levels and the Level 3 experience requirement for the license. Rule 62-602.270 is an older current rule version, effective October 15, 2007, but it remains listed by Florida’s official rules repository and states the current examination-entry prerequisites for Level 3. [3] [4]

The DEP distribution-licensing page was last modified July 30, 2025 and says new applicants must begin at Level 3. The official Level 3 examination outline remains hosted by DEP and is linked from DEP’s examination page, but the outline itself does not display a publication or revision date. It is treated as a current official content-boundary source because DEP currently publishes and links it; its missing revision date is a freshness limitation. [1] [2] [5]

There is no unresolved authority conflict for the Level 3 route. The apparent Level 4 issue is handled as a boundary: the outline and current licensure rule retain Level 4 references, while DEP’s licensing page directs new applicants to start at Level 3. This dossier relies only on the common, explicit Level 3 boundary and makes no claim about availability, transition status, or equivalency of a Level 4 route. [1] [3] [5]
