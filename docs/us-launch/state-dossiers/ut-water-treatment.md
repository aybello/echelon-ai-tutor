# Utah water-treatment operator-certification route

## Route and decision

- **Dossier ID:** `ut-water-treatment`
- **State / stream:** Utah (UT) — drinking-water treatment
- **Matrix label:** State-specific
- **Readiness decision:** **Narrow**
- **Permitted future boundary:** Utah Division of Drinking Water (DDW) **Water Treatment Plant Operator — Treatment Grade I (T1)** certification examination only.

Utah’s current program separates the treatment discipline into four grades. The official DDW examination page and study-help outline identify a T1 treatment examination with its own grade-specific content distribution; the current rule also recognizes four water-treatment grades. The full state-stream route must not be represented as a single generic treatment exam or as a bank covering T1–T4. A limited T1-only candidate dossier is sufficiently identified for future review-only work. [1] [2] [3]

## Credential and examination boundary

The credential discipline is **Water Treatment Plant Operator** under the Utah DDW Operator Certification Program. The selected examination boundary is **Treatment Grade I**. DDW identifies T1 as the treatment grade associated with public water systems serving 25–1,500 population, while the rule states that treatment Grade I represents the smallest public-water-supply knowledge and experience level within the treatment discipline. [1] [2] [3]

For this boundary, DDW states that the certification examination has 100 multiple-choice questions, is timed and proctored, and requires a 70% passing score. The official T1 outline allocates the 100 questions across six categories: math (15), operation and maintenance (25), rules (15), pumps (10), chemical feed (15), and safety and security (20). [1] [2]

Passing an examination supports a restricted certificate when the candidate lacks the required experience; unrestricted status depends on the applicable experience and other certification requirements. This dossier therefore concerns the knowledge examination only. It does not assert that passing a future practice candidate, or the state examination, independently confers unrestricted status, Direct Responsible Charge status, reciprocity, employment eligibility, or system-operating authority. [3]

## Source freshness and conflicts

The DDW pages were retrieved from the current Utah DEQ website on 2026-10-29. Their linked study assets carry DDW 2025 identifiers, which supports use of the page as a current program publication. The rule is marked **Current Rule**, effective November 9, 2017, with a Notice of Continuation dated February 10, 2025. Its age is a limitation for detailed instructional claims, but it remains the current legal source for the credential framework. [3]

No material conflict was found. The DDW pages give the detailed operational exam format and current T1 category distribution, while R309-300 provides the legal certification structure and broader examination areas. The two levels of source are complementary. Where they differ in granularity, the current DDW study-help allocation controls the T1 candidate boundary and the rule controls legal credential claims.

## Learner-level scope and exclusions

The learner level is an applicant preparing for the **Utah Water Treatment Plant Operator Treatment Grade I** knowledge examination. The future candidate dossier may address only foundational, source-traceable concepts within the six DDW T1 categories. It must remain limited to the published T1 examination structure, not generic water-operator content.

Excluded from this route are Treatment Grades II–IV; distribution and small-system examinations; wastewater operator credentials; reciprocity; restricted-to-unrestricted experience determinations; Direct Responsible Charge qualifications; system classification determinations; site-specific operating procedures; engineering or process design; advanced treatment material associated by DDW with T3–T4; and unverified third-party or commercial-preparation content. [1] [3]
