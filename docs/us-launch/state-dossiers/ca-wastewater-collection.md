## Decision

**Suitable** for a limited, future **review-only** candidate dossier at the single boundary of **CWEA CSM Grade 1**. The currently posted official handbook is Version 2025.01, effective May 2025, and the official program page identifies it as the handbook for exams beginning July 1, 2025. It contains a separate Grade 1 examination content outline, grade-specific role definition, domain weights, and explicit scope. CWEA states that its collection-system certifications are specific to California. [1] [2]

This is a voluntary professional certification, not California’s mandatory State Water Resources Control Board wastewater-treatment-plant operator certificate. The latter has five grades and applies to operators of wastewater treatment plants, so it must not be combined with this collection-system route. [1] [5] [6]

## Credential and examination boundary

The eligible future package boundary is only:

> **California Water Environment Association Technical Certification Program — Collection System Maintenance Grade 1 examination, using Candidate Handbook v2025.01 for exams launched July 1, 2025.**

CWEA characterizes CSM Grade 1 as the entry and basic working level. The handbook describes the expected knowledge and ability as basic recall, recognition, comprehension, and application needed to perform basic tasks safely and effectively; the role receives direct supervision. Grade 1 has no experience requirement, although CWEA recommends about one year of vocational experience. [2]

The official Grade 1 outline allocates the examination across six domains: collection-system operations and maintenance (28%); tools and equipment (20%); collection-system mapping (5%); safety and regulations (26%); administration (11%); and collection-system math (10%). [2]

This boundary does **not** encompass CSM Grades 2–4. CWEA defines Grade 2 as journey-level, Grade 3 as lead/advanced technical, and Grade 4 as program-manager level; each has a separate official outline and more advanced responsibility. [2]

## Learner-level scope and exclusions

A future candidate dossier at this boundary may address only the Grade 1 outline at an entry-level, direct-supervision standard. High-level, source-bound objectives are:

- Recognize foundational collection-system operations and maintenance concepts for gravity systems, pumps or force mains, and initial spill-response duties.
- Identify basic inspection, maintenance, repair, and locating tools or equipment and interpret collection-system maps at the Grade 1 level.
- Recognize the Grade 1 safety-and-regulation concepts identified in the outline, including jobsite hazards, confined-space awareness, utility-location awareness, traffic control, trenching or shoring awareness, and hazardous-material safety.
- Recognize basic work-record, documentation, customer-service, and public-communication responsibilities for collection-system work.
- Apply the outline’s foundational measurement and calculation categories for collection-system work.

The following are excluded from this route and any future Grade 1 candidate package:

- CWEA CSM Grades 2, 3, and 4, including journey-level troubleshooting, supervisory duties, program management, advanced technical analysis, policy development, regulatory reporting, and program assurance.
- The State Water Resources Control Board mandatory wastewater treatment plant operator program, including its Grades I–V, operator-in-training route, treatment-process operation, and treatment-plant certification requirements.
- Drinking-water treatment or distribution credentials; laboratory, electrical, mechanical, and environmental-compliance credentials; and non-California certification programs.
- Site-specific operating instructions; an employer’s emergency plan, permits, work orders, or local agency policy; and hands-on competency verification.
- Safety-critical procedural instruction, regulatory thresholds, reporting volumes or timeframes, and legal determinations unless a future item is separately tied to the then-current primary authority and independently reviewed by an appropriately qualified reviewer.

## Source freshness and conflict assessment

The principal examination source is current enough for candidate-package planning: CWEA’s Version 2025.01 handbook is explicitly effective May 2025, its CSM program page labels it for examinations beginning July 1, 2025, CWEA’s candidate-materials page continues to post it at retrieval, and CWEA describes the 2025 revalidation and revised-blueprint launch. The route must therefore retain the exact CSM Grade 1 label and must not be represented as State Water Board wastewater operator certification. [1] [2] [5] [6]

Freshness limitation: the official CSM content outline is current as posted but was issued for the 2025 revised exam. Before any later candidate drafting, the owner must reconfirm that CWEA still posts Version 2025.01 or obtain the replacement handbook and revalidate the examination boundary and objectives. A material edition change, changed domain weighting, or revised regulatory source requires a new readiness review.
