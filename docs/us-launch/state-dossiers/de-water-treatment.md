## Decision

**Narrow** — Delaware has a current, state-administered written **Base-Level Water Operator** examination and separately registered treatment-endorsement examinations. The state’s current rule, active registration form, licence application, and September 2026 Office of Drinking Water programme report establish a defensible, bounded foundation for the *treatment-oriented portion* of the Base-Level Water Operator/OIT pathway. They do **not** publish a current exam blueprint, candidate handbook, domain weights, pass-by-domain rules, or specific endorsed-treatment exam outlines. [1] [3] [4] [6]

This route is therefore not ready as a full Delaware water-treatment bank. It is suitable only for a future, review-only **narrow candidate dossier** tied to the base-level written exam and limited to the programme-supported foundational treatment areas below. Under the governing readiness contract, only a later **Suitable** dossier may generate review-only JSON candidate batches; this dossier does not authorize such a batch. No learner access follows from this research decision.

## Credential and examination boundary

The credentialing authority is the **Delaware Department of Health and Social Services, Division of Public Health, Office of Drinking Water (ODW)**, working through the Advisory Council for Certification of Public Water System Operators. The applicable live rule is **16 Delaware Administrative Code 4463, Licensing and Registration of Operators of Public Water Supply Systems**. [1] [2]

The narrow boundary is:

> **Delaware Base-Level Water Operator written examination, including the Operator-in-Training pathway — treatment-oriented foundational subset only.**

A base-level applicant needs the applicable education/operating-experience route and successful completion of the base-level written examination. A water-treatment OIT may take that written examination before completing the base-level education or experience requirements, subject to the stated OIT conditions. [1]

The rule defines the base-level license as covering general water-system information, hypochlorination disinfection, and lower-flow distribution operation and maintenance. The current ODW licence application more specifically says the base-level license includes hypochlorination disinfection, lower-flow distribution, and Approved Sampler/Tester endorsements. [1] [4] The registration form separately lets applicants select the base-level exam or examinations labelled Disinfection, Chemical Feed, Filtration, Surface Water, Distribution, and Other Treatments. [3]

This dossier deliberately excludes the distribution component of the mixed base-level exam. It also does not treat an endorsement label as an endorsed-process examination outline.

## Source freshness, completeness, and conflicts

The primary rule is a live entry in Delaware’s current Administrative Code. Its displayed amendment history includes **23 DE Reg. 928, effective May 1, 2020**. The ODW certification page currently links to that rule and the 2023 registration and application forms. The 2026 ODW report is the newest programme source identified and explicitly describes the base-level training as certification-exam preparation. [1] [2] [3] [4] [6]

No current official candidate handbook, examination-content outline, blueprint, domain weighting, list of scored competencies, or endorsed-treatment examination outline was located on the active ODW certification page, ODW page, or in the official Delaware-source searches conducted for this dossier. The publicly available current sources identify exam names and broad course areas, but not the assessed depth or allocation of those areas. That limitation prevents a full-route **Suitable** decision. [2] [5]

There is a material source conflict outside this dossier’s narrow treatment boundary. Rule 4463 describes the base-level threshold and certain distribution requirements at **500 gpm at 20 psi**, while the linked 2023 application and examination-registration forms display lower- and higher-flow distribution references at **25 psi**. [1] [3] [4] No future treatment-subset material may test, teach, or state either flow/pressure threshold unless ODW supplies a current controlling clarification. Distribution content is excluded here.

The rule lists broad endorsement categories and subcategories, but it also says Appendix A may be amended with changes in treatment technologies. These lists establish credential structure, not a current examinable blueprint. [1]

## Learner level, supported scope, and exclusions

**Learner level:** Prospective Delaware Base-Level Water Operator applicants and treatment OIT applicants preparing for the written base-level examination. The evidence does not support treating this as an advanced operator, direct-responsible-charge, full distribution-license, or individual treatment-endorsement preparation programme. [1] [3] [4]

**Supported scope:** Foundational, treatment-oriented base-level concepts only, within the high-level areas that ODW says the entry/base-level course teaches in preparation for the state certification exam. These areas are not exam weights or a published blueprint. [6]

**Explicit exclusions:**

- The Distribution License examination and all distribution operation content, including the conflicting flow/pressure thresholds.
- Individual endorsement or sub-endorsement examination preparation for chemical feed, filtration, surface water, other specified treatment, or advanced disinfection processes, because no official outline for those exams was located.
- Plant-specific operating procedures, process-control setpoints, chemical dosages, emergency response steps, laboratory methods, design, installation, maintenance instructions, or other safety-critical procedures not established by a current official examination outline.
- Licence eligibility, employment, direct-responsible-charge, reciprocity, renewal, disciplinary, and reporting rules as candidate content, except where later needed to delimit the credential with direct current-source support.
- Any claim that a listed course topic is weighted, guaranteed to be tested, or sufficient to obtain a Delaware licence.

## Bank recommendation

**No unique bank is proposed.** The evidence supports only a narrow, mixed-exam base-level treatment subset, not a discrete Delaware water-treatment credential or a published endorsed-treatment blueprint. Creating a bank identity now would risk implying a full Delaware treatment or endorsement-exam coverage that the official sources do not support.

Before any new bank is considered, obtain a current official base-level examination outline or direct written confirmation from ODW/its Advisory Council of the treatment domains actually assessed. Also complete the required read-only recovery and collision check against the existing U.S. Class I package identities before creating anything that might overlap shared foundational water content. A later reassessment may decide whether the evidence supports a distinct state addition rather than a duplicate bank.
