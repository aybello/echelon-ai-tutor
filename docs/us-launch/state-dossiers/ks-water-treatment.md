# Kansas Water-Treatment Operator Certification Readiness Dossier

**Route ID:** `ks-water-treatment`
**Existing matrix label:** `state-specific`
**Research retrieval date:** October 29, 2026
**Decision:** **Defer**

## Exact state and stream

**Kansas (KS) — water treatment.** For this route, “water treatment” is limited to treatment of a public water-supply system. It excludes wastewater treatment, water distribution as a standalone stream, wastewater collection, laboratory certification, and non-Kansas credentials.

Kansas does not issue a separately named “water-treatment operator” credential. KDHE administers a **water-supply-system operator** certification programme with Small System and Classes I–IV. Under the active classification rule, treatment is a component of the water-supply-system credential and includes iron and manganese removal, membrane filtration, softening, coagulation/sedimentation/filtration, recarbonation, and chemical addition other than chlorine. The same credential family can also encompass distribution-only and chlorination-only systems. [1] [4]

## Credential and examination boundary

The verified credential family is **Kansas Department of Health and Environment water-supply-system operator certification**, at one of five certificate classes: Small System, I, II, III, or IV. Applicants must meet class-specific points and experience requirements and pass the appropriate written examination. KDHE’s active rule sets a passing score of 70 percent and requires the Department to offer examinations at least twice annually. [2] [3]

The active rule connects facility classification to certificate class. For treated water-supply systems, the class varies by groundwater or surface-water treatment and population served: Class I applies to treatment of groundwater serving fewer than 501 people; Class II to groundwater treatment serving 501–2,500 people or surface-water treatment serving fewer than 2,501 people; Class III to groundwater or surface-water treatment serving 2,501–10,000 people; and Class IV to groundwater or surface-water treatment serving more than 10,000 people. [4]

This confirms a live Kansas examination family, but not an examination boundary for the assigned treatment-only stream. The current programme does not publicly identify a separately titled water-treatment examination, nor does the active rule assign examination content or objectives to a treatment-only credential. Instead, the available certificate/examination family is the broader water-supply-system operator programme. [1] [2] [3] [4]

**Boundary finding:** A future package cannot presently be described as preparation for a Kansas water-treatment-only exam. At most, a later dossier could consider one named **Kansas water-supply-system operator class** after KDHE supplies or confirms a current class-specific examination outline that also distinguishes the applicable treatment scope from the broader water-supply-system scope.

## Source freshness and conflicts

The regulatory and programme existence evidence is current: the Kansas regulations portal marks the relevant rules as active, KDHE’s live programme page currently exposes operator information and water “Need to Know” resources, and the Legislative Post Audit report was published in February 2025. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]

The material limitation is not a direct conflict among current authorities. It is the absence of a current, specific, publicly available Kansas examination blueprint for one water-treatment class. KDHE’s currently linked detailed water knowledge documents are all dated April 22, 2009. They are water-operator documents, rather than treatment-only outlines, and each disclaims use as a sole certification-exam study guide. [1] [6]

The 2025 official audit records that KDHE reviewed water-supply examination questions and made language updates in 2021. That programme history means the 2009 lists cannot safely be assumed to be the current, complete examination specification. The audit also does not publish the updated test plan, a candidate handbook, objective weights, or a treatment-only class/exam crosswalk. [5]

Accordingly, no detailed topic list, topic weighting, mathematical formula scope, regulatory threshold, or operating-procedure claim from the 2009 documents may be carried forward as a current Kansas examination requirement.

## Learner-level scope and exclusions

The only defensible future learner population would be a Kansas applicant who is eligible for one specifically named **water-supply-system operator** class and whose chosen class is verified to cover a treated groundwater or surface-water system under K.A.R. 28-16-36. This is an examination-preparation boundary only. It does not determine candidate eligibility, award certification, establish competence to operate a facility, or replace KDHE’s experience, points, employment, or education requirements. [2] [3] [4]

Until KDHE publishes or confirms a current class-specific outline, all of the following remain excluded:

- a generic Kansas “water treatment” bank or any claim that Kansas offers a separate water-treatment-only examination;
- all objectives and knowledge statements drawn from the 2009 “Need to Know” PDFs;
- distribution, pumping, source-water protection, cross-connection, management, sampling, reporting, laboratory, safety, and regulatory content unless a current KDHE class-specific examination outline expressly supports the precise objective;
- wastewater-treatment, wastewater-collection, laboratory, distribution-only, and other non-water-supply credential content;
- cross-class content and inferences that a process listed in a facility-classification rule is examined at a particular class;
- eligibility decisions, experience/points calculations, reciprocity, certificate renewal, scheduling, and test-administration procedures; and
- facility design, site-specific operations, emergency response, detailed safety procedures, or compliance thresholds.

No high-level objectives are stated because a **Defer** decision does not support objective selection.

## Readiness decision

**Defer.** Kansas has a verified, active, state-administered water-supply-system operator certification programme with class-specific written examinations. The current rules provide a clear legal/examination framework and define which treated systems correspond to certificate classes. [2] [3] [4]

The route is nevertheless not ready for a future review-only question-bank candidate package. The exact assigned stream is water treatment, while the verified Kansas credential and examination family is broader water-supply-system operation. KDHE has not publicly supplied a current candidate handbook, class-specific examination outline, or explicit current crosswalk that limits an examination to water treatment. The only detailed knowledge lists publicly linked by KDHE are 2009 documents that expressly are not sole exam study guides, and the 2025 official audit documents a 2021 language update to water-supply examination questions. [1] [5] [6]

This decision does not find that Kansas lacks operator certification or treatment-related examinations. It finds that the current public official evidence does not safely establish one bounded, current, treatment-only test specification from which even a limited candidate dossier could be authored.

## Bank recommendation

**No unique bank proposed.** Do not create `ks-water-treatment`, a generic Kansas water-treatment bank, a Kansas water-supply bank, or a class-named bank based on the current record. Such an identity could misrepresent the credential boundary, capture distribution or other non-treatment content, and duplicate an unverified broader programme scope.

Reconsider only after KDHE provides or publicly posts, for one named water-supply-system certificate class, a current examination outline or candidate handbook that identifies the assessed treatment scope and its relationship to the class examination. The later dossier must then confirm whether a proposed class-specific package overlaps an existing reviewed U.S. or shared package before any bank identity is recommended.

## Candidate-only / no-learner-access boundary

This is a research-only readiness dossier. It authorizes **no candidate package, no question authoring, no bank data, no database action, and no learner-facing material**.

If the missing current, class-specific official examination boundary is later obtained and independently verified, any subsequent work remains **candidate-only / review-only / no learner access**. It must stay non-public and may proceed only with a source-bound evidence record, structural validation, independent review, and the required approval controls. Nothing in this dossier authorizes staging, activation, publication, sale, delivery, or release.
