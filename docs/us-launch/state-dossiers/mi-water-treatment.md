# Michigan — Water Treatment Readiness Dossier

**Dossier ID:** `mi-water-treatment`
**State / stream:** Michigan (MI) / drinking-water treatment
**Matrix label:** State-specific
**Research status:** Official-source readiness review only
**Retrieved:** October 29, 2026
**Decision:** **Narrow**

Michigan has a current, state-administered drinking-water operator-certification program with a specific treatment credential family and published examination scope. The broad matrix stream “water treatment,” however, contains separate F Complete Treatment, D Limited Treatment, and F/D Level 5 noncommunity pathways. This dossier therefore supports only the bounded **Type I F Complete Treatment, F-3 and F-4 written certification-examination** subset. It does not support a single bank for all Michigan water-treatment credentials. [1] [2] [3]

## Credential and exam boundary

The governing credential is Michigan EGLE’s **Type I F — Complete Treatment** operator certification at **F-3 or F-4**. Part 19 classifies F-3 community complete-treatment systems as those serving 1,000 to 4,000 people or having 0.5 to 2.0 million gallons per day of rated treatment capacity, and F-4 systems as those serving fewer than 1,000 people or having less than 0.5 million gallons per day of rated treatment capacity. The department may reclassify a system for specific complexity or operational factors, so the class descriptions are a certification-system boundary, not a guarantee of an individual system’s assigned class. [1]

For this bounded route, the relevant assessment boundary is the EGLE-administered **written F-3/F-4 certification examination**. The administrative rule requires a department-prepared written examination for each public-water-supply classification other than Level 5, with Advisory Board concurrence; it also permits a performance-based laboratory examination. EGLE’s current requirements state that first-time or lapsed F applicants take a written laboratory examination and, after passing the F and written laboratory examinations, are contacted to schedule a practical laboratory examination. A future candidate package under this dossier may address only the written F-3/F-4 exam-content scope. It must not represent itself as preparation for, or evidence of completion of, the laboratory, practical, education, experience, application, approval, or certification processes. [1] [3]

EGLE’s current application page lists F — Complete Treatment Levels 1–5 and offers F 1–4 exams on the 2026 May and November schedule. The applicable 2026 requirements document separately publishes subject groups for Level 3 and 4 F examinations. [2] [3]

## Learner-level scope and exclusions

**In scope:** prospective or approved Michigan community-public-water-supply operator candidates preparing for the written F-3 or F-4 Complete Treatment certification examination. This is a state-specific professional credential scope, not general drinking-water instruction.

**Excluded:**

- F-1 and F-2 Complete Treatment examinations, which have their own level grouping in the official study guide.
- F-5 noncommunity certification, for which Part 19 permits a different mix of training, written, oral, or performance-based assessment.
- D Limited Treatment certification at any level, including D-3/D-4, despite some overlap in technical subjects.
- S Distribution certification at any level.
- Site-specific restricted certificates, reciprocity, continuing-education renewal, eligibility scoring, operating-experience verification, laboratory/practical examination performance, and facility-specific operating instructions.
- Any state reporting threshold, compliance deadline, design prescription, or safety procedure not re-verified in the current controlling source during later item-level review.

## Source freshness and conflicts

The controlling legal source is the current Part 19 administrative-rules publication. The current EGLE application page, Rev. 2/2026 certification-requirements document, and Rev. 3/2026 F practice resource all show active 2026 program administration. They provide enough current official support for a bounded F-3/F-4 written-exam candidate dossier. [1] [2] [3] [7]

The official F1–F4 study guide remains posted by EGLE but is marked “Revised 7/19.” It is therefore useful to distinguish level organization but is not the lead authority for newly authored content. The Rev. 2/2026 requirements document is the controlling current content source for the F-3/F-4 topic family, and it cautions that subjects may change without notice. Any later item-level work must re-check the live EGLE page and current requirements document before review. [3] [6]

There is a terminology difference, not an assessment conflict: the current application and material pages call F “Complete Treatment,” while the requirements document refers to an F “Filtration” examination category. Part 19 classifies F systems as Complete Treatment systems, and the current application explicitly labels the credential F — Complete Treatment. This dossier uses **F Complete Treatment** as the controlling credential name and does not treat “Filtration” as a separate credential. [1] [2] [3]

## High-level objective boundary for a future candidate dossier

The following objective families are permitted only at a high level for the Type I F-3/F-4 written-exam boundary:

1. Relate complete-treatment process concepts to source-water characteristics and finished-water treatment goals.
2. Apply foundational treatment-process concepts across coagulation and flocculation, disinfection, filtration, softening, and related water-quality concerns.
3. Apply general treatment-plant operation and maintenance concepts involving hydraulics, pumping, instrumentation, and routine process monitoring.
4. Interpret the role of laboratory work, microbiology, sampling, and recordkeeping in complete-treatment operations.
5. Recognize the Michigan F-3/F-4 exam’s high-level regulatory, cross-connection, contingency, management, and safety subject areas without substituting generalized content for current Michigan rules. [3] [6]

These are source-bound objective families only. They are not questions, answers, explanations, calculations, a candidate batch, or a learner-facing study product.

## Bank recommendation

A single, future unique state-specific bank is supportable only for **Michigan Type I F Complete Treatment, F-3/F-4 written examination**. The recommended scope name is **Michigan F Complete Treatment F-3/F-4**. This is a recommendation for a future review-only candidate package, not the creation of a bank, candidate record, or content release. No broader Michigan water-treatment bank is recommended because it would improperly combine Complete Treatment, Limited Treatment, distribution, noncommunity Level 5, and higher F-level boundaries.
