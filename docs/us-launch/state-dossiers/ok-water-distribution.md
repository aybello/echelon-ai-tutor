# Oklahoma — water distribution

**Route ID:** `ok-water-distribution`
**State / stream:** Oklahoma (OK) / water-distribution
**Existing matrix label:** State-specific
**Dossier date:** October 29, 2026
**Decision:** **Narrow**

Oklahoma has a current, state-administered **Distribution and Collection** certification examination family. It does **not** identify a water-distribution-only credential or examination. The assigned water-distribution route is therefore supportable only as the potable-water distribution portion of the combined Oklahoma credential, not as a standalone Oklahoma water-distribution bank. The currently published DEQ guide supplies topic-level material for that subset, but it is a legacy document and retains a technician designation that differs from the current rule and application. This route is not yet ready to create a review-only candidate package under the readiness contract, which reserves candidate batches for **Suitable** dossiers.

## Credential and examination boundary

The governing boundary is the **Oklahoma Department of Environmental Quality (DEQ) Distribution and Collection Operator, Class C**, associated with application exam number **100129**, and the **Distribution and Collection Technician, Class T**, associated with application exam number **100130**. The current rule makes both certifications available and requires an appropriate validated examination. It defines a distribution system as equipment delivering water from source or treatment facilities to consumers, while a collection system conveys wastewater. The same rule expressly authorizes both certifications to perform duties for storage and distribution systems **and** total-retention-lagoon and collection systems. [1] [2]

Accordingly, this is one combined state credential/exam family spanning water distribution and wastewater collection. There is no official evidence of a separate Oklahoma water-distribution-only examination, a distribution-only candidate handbook, or a current distribution-only examination outline. The official study guide describes preparation for the combined technician/operator examinations and contains separate chapters for distribution and collection; it does not convert the distribution chapter into a distinct credential boundary. [4]

## Official source record

1. **Oklahoma Administrative Code, Chapter 710, Waterworks and Wastewater Works Operator Certification** — issuer: Oklahoma Department of Environmental Quality; retrieved October 29, 2026; current rule text records amendments effective September 15, 2025. This is the controlling source for the applicability of the program; definitions of distribution system, collection system, and validated examination; the Class C Distribution and Collection Operator and Class T Distribution and Collection Technician; the requirement to pass a validated examination; and the combined scope of their authorized duties. [1]

2. **DEQ Water and Wastewater Works Operator Exam Application, Form 710-001, Revision 09/25** — issuer: Oklahoma Department of Environmental Quality; retrieved October 29, 2026. The form lists distinct current application selections for Distribution/Collection **C Operator (100129)** and **Technician (100130)**. It gives the current application qualification chart: 36 training hours and one year of experience for the C Operator; eight training hours and no experience for the Technician. It also states that a DEQ approval letter is required before an applicant may take an examination. [2]

3. **Operator Certification — Program Information** — issuer: Oklahoma Department of Environmental Quality, Water Quality Division / Operator Certification Unit; retrieved October 29, 2026. The current program page says that DEQ’s Operator Certification Unit tests and certifies Oklahoma operators and continues to publish the official **Distribution/Collection Manual** among its forms and study guides. This confirms the guide remains an agency-provided program resource, but does not represent it as a current exam blueprint or revise its legacy class terminology. [3]

4. **Distribution/Collection Manual** — issuer: Oklahoma Department of Environmental Quality; retrieved October 29, 2026. The official guide says it is for Oklahoma distribution/collection technician and operator certification, says that examination categories correspond to its chapters or sections, and gives water-distribution study guidelines for its Chapter 2. The guide calls the technician “Class D” and the operator “Class C”; its PDF metadata indicates creation and modification in June 2010. It also states that it is not a technical operating manual and cannot supersede later statutes, rules, regulations, standards, or legal requirements. It is usable only as bounded, topic-level evidence after current legal and examination alignment is confirmed. [4]

## Source freshness and conflicts

The current rule and revision-09/25 examination application are the controlling sources. They align on a combined Distribution and Collection credential family and identify the current technician as **Class T**, with a C-level operator certification. [1] [2]

The DEQ program page currently links the Distribution/Collection Manual, so the manual remains official program information. However, the guide’s June 2010 PDF metadata and its repeated **Class D** technician designation conflict with the current Class T designation in the rule. The guide also says it cannot supersede subsequently effective legal requirements. Its detailed content therefore cannot establish current legal thresholds, current construction requirements, or a complete current test blueprint. [1] [3] [4]

No conflict was found about the existence of the combined examination family. The material limitation is structural: the current official credential is combined distribution/collection, while this assignment is water-distribution only. A separate water-distribution examination boundary cannot be inferred from a chapter inside a combined guide.

## Learner-level scope and exclusions

The only supportable future instructional scope is a limited potable-water-distribution subset of the combined Oklahoma Distribution and Collection exam family. It may distinguish the entry technician concepts from the additional Class C operator concepts, but it must not represent either as an Oklahoma water-distribution-only credential.

For a future, separately approved source record, high-level objectives could be limited to:

- Describe the basic purpose, components, and operating context of potable-water distribution systems and storage.
- Recognize foundational distribution-system concepts concerning pipe characteristics, line location and separation concepts, installation context, and common distribution-system problems.
- Identify high-level water-distribution concepts involving storage, valves, meters, cross-connection control, and distribution water-quality protection.
- Apply foundational, source-supported calculations involving flow, pressure, volume, and line or tank disinfection context at the Class C scope.
- Recognize general preventive-maintenance and worker-safety concepts relevant to distribution equipment, without giving site-specific operating instructions.

Excluded from this route are wastewater collection, total-retention lagoons, wastewater treatment, water-treatment-process content, laboratory certification, Class A/B waterworks material, technician or operator eligibility determinations beyond the cited current application, facility-specific SOPs, field procedures, and legal/compliance thresholds drawn solely from the legacy guide. The source set also does not support reproducing or inferring confidential validated examination content.

## Decision and bank recommendation

**Narrow.** The state, regulator, live combined credential/exam family, and a water-distribution topic subset are verified. The route fails the standard for **Suitable** because the officially current credential is not water-distribution-only and the only detailed distribution topic document is a legacy guide with an obsolete technician designation and an express non-supersession warning. A current DEQ examination outline, candidate handbook, or written DEQ confirmation that maps the live Class T/C examinations to the guide would be needed before reassessing the route as Suitable.

**Bank recommendation:** Do **not** propose a unique `ok-water-distribution` bank. The official evidence supports only a combined Oklahoma Distribution and Collection credential family, not a discrete water-distribution product. A combined-family bank should not be proposed from this single-stream dossier either; it would require a separate combined-scope source review, a current official blueprint or DEQ confirmation, and reconciliation against any existing U.S. Class I package before authoring.
