## Modules
1. **Hazard recognition and personal protective equipment:** finding hazards before work, the hierarchy of controls (eliminate, substitute, engineer, administer, PPE), PPE types and what each protects against, hazard communication, labels and safety data sheets (what each section tells an operator), housekeeping, slips, trips and falls, working alone.
2. **Confined spaces and atmospheric hazards (recognition and purpose only):** what makes a space confined, why atmospheres change (low oxygen, hydrogen sulfide, methane and other flammable gas, chlorine), why testing, ventilation, an attendant and a rescue plan exist, why permits exist, why you never rely on smell, rescue awareness (do not become the second victim). No step-by-step entry procedure, no exact numbers.
3. **Excavations, traffic, energy and lifting:** why trenches cave in, what shoring, sloping and shielding are for, ladders and spoil piles, traffic control purpose, electrical hazards and why lockout and tagout exist (purpose and recognition, not steps), arc and shock awareness, lifting and ergonomics, hand and power tools, noise.
4. **Chemical safety and emergency awareness:** chemical hazards common in treatment and distribution (chlorine gas, hypochlorite, ammonia, caustic, acids, polymer, fluoride compounds), incompatible chemicals and why they are stored apart, cylinder and container handling awareness, spill and leak recognition and who to notify, first aid awareness, eyewash and shower purpose, fire awareness, emergency plans and drills, reporting near misses, facility security awareness.

## Suggested diagrams
Hierarchy-of-controls pyramid; PPE zones on a body outline; confined-space atmosphere hazard map (what rises, what sinks); trench protection (sloped, shored, shielded); chemical incompatibility storage layout.

## Question mix
At least 20 percent of final questions are scenario-recognition items; calculations are optional (for example oxygen percentage readings, simple unit conversions) and must follow the calculation rules.
