# Source check: ceu-sludge-digestion vs WEF MOP 11 (6th ed.) Chapters 30 to 32 and the Wisconsin Advanced Wastewater Study Guide

Checked 2026-11-26 while drafting, against the pdftotext extractions of Chapter 30 (Anaerobic Digestion), Chapters 31 and 32 (Aerobic Digestion, Additional Stabilization Methods) and the Wisconsin DNR Advanced Wastewater Study Guide (February 2016). Page markers are as printed: WEF pages as 30-x, 31-x, 32-x taken from the running page header that precedes the quoted text, Wisconsin pages as the printed page number at the foot of the page (the quote sits on that page; for items that run over two pages the start page is given). Every quote in the Evidence column was located in the extracted text by a script (whitespace and hyphenation at line breaks ignored), so each quote is verbatim; where a claim lists several items, the quote shows one representative sentence and the other items sit in the same passage. Spellman (sp5, sp6) was not used.

Verdicts: SUPPORTED (the source says it), CONTRADICTED (the source says otherwise or the draft overstated it), NOT COVERED (the sources are silent; the claim is general, arithmetic, or a course activity). "Arithmetic only" rows are calculations whose formula is general arithmetic; they are counted under NOT COVERED and every one was recomputed with a separate `python3 -I` script (27 calculation questions in all: 24 arithmetic only, plus 3 whose formula the source gives, shown as SUPPORTED).

The CONTRADICTED rows are slips found and corrected during drafting. Their wording is the first-draft wording, and the final course text already carries the fix. Sections: Totals, then claims by module.

## Source inconsistencies noticed (not used in the course)

- Mesophilic range: 32 to 38 C in Process Overview and Table 30.7 (30-4, 30-54), 30 to 38 C in Monitoring and Testing, Temperature (30-63). The course uses 32 to 38 C.
- Flammable range of methane: 5 to 15 percent in Table 30.2 (30-37) and 5 to 20 percent in Safety (30-50); Table 30.6 says 4 to 44 percent. No range is stated in the course.
- Digester gas typical production: 12 to 18 cu ft per lb VS destroyed in the text (30-37) and 12 to 16 in Table 30.7 (30-54). The course gives 12 to 18 and uses 15 in two prompts, where it is stated.
- Volatile acids typical range: 50 to 300 mg/L in text (30-64) and 50 to 330 in Table 30.7. No range is used.
- The extraction of 30-63 reads "the tank temperature must deviate from that value by more than 0.6 C (1 F) per day"; a "not" has been lost in the extraction, so that sentence was not quoted.
- Wisconsin guide 22.3.1 heading says aerobic-anoxic operation of an "anaerobic digester"; the content is about aerobic digesters.
- ATAD retention: about 5 to 6 days (MOP 11, 31-8) against 6 to 9 days (Wisconsin 22.3.2) and 6 to 8 typical in Table 31.2. The course quotes both sources by name.
