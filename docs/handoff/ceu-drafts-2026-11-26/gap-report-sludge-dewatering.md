# Gap report: Sludge Dewatering vs the Royce U equivalent (course code sdwp)

Read 2026-11-26. Royce U Sludge Dewatering (sdwp): all 205 slides extracted from `royce/sdwp/data/s<N>.js` (text from span elements) and read, slide 1 to 205. The course is a belt filter press course with study questions after each section. Our course covers more machine types and the plant-side records, so the gap is mostly in belt press detail.

Rules followed: nothing copied from slide wording, questions, examples, images or numbers; none of their numeric limits, set points or step sizes are restated; every added item comes from WEF MOP 11 Chapter 33, the Wisconsin Advanced Wastewater Study Guide or Spellman Vol II, with our own scenarios and numbers.
