# Gap report: Sludge Thickening vs Royce U `stp` (359 slides, text read in full 2026-11-26)

Topics only, in our own words. No Royce wording, questions, examples, numbers or images are reused. Royce U covers two machines: gravity belt thickening (GBT) and dissolved air flotation (DAF). It has no gravity (circular) thickener or centrifuge section, no mass-balance or percent-solids arithmetic, so our draft is broader in those areas. About 25 slides are image-only (troubleshooting and flow diagrams); their topics were taken from neighbouring text slides, so those items are marked "inferred".

## Not added (no textbook support, or plant-specific)

## Not to add (jurisdiction-specific or regulatory)
- Any target percent solids, solids loading limit, polymer dose range, wash-water quality limit or air-to-solids target as a rule: these are design or permit values set by the manufacturer, plant manual or agency. The draft quotes textbook ranges only as source attributions.
- Royce U's numeric operating ranges and the example arithmetic: not reused. Our new calculations use our own numbers.
- Regulatory limits on filtrate or return flows, biosolids rules, or state certification exam content: leave to the learner's own agency.
- Manufacturer-specific procedures (named hydraulic settings, belt widths, rated capacities).
