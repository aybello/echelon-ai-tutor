# Gap report: Reverse Osmosis vs Royce U `rop` (274 slides, text read in full 2026-11-26)

Topics only, in our own words. No Royce wording, questions, examples, numbers or images are reused; their numeric limits (SDI cap, cleaning triggers, antiscalant doses, flux rates, pressures) are not restated. Royce U covers membrane treatment as a whole (MF, UF, NF and RO) with a Florida bias; our course is RO-focused and adds the performance arithmetic (recovery, rejection, salt passage, flux, concentration factor, osmotic pressure, pump energy, normalised trending), which `rop` mostly lacks. About 40 slides are image-only (charts, formulas, photographs); their topics were taken from the neighbouring text slides.

Draft compared: `ceudrafts/ceu-reverse-osmosis.json` version 2026-11-26.1. Backup of the pre-gap build is not kept separately because the closure edits were made in the lesson sources before first publish; the pre-gap state is the lesson files minus the additions listed below.
