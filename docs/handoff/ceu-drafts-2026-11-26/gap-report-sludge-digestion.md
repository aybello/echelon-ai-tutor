## What was added to the course
- M1: Van Kleeck method named, fixed (ash) share arithmetic, feed type and process choice from WEF.
- M2: primary and secondary digesters, scum, flame traps and relief valves at recognition level, key factors list, carbon dioxide share as a check, a new figure of VA:Alk zones.
- M3: SRT from the mass of solids with a worked example, decanting and supernatant, carbon dioxide and nitrate lowering pH, neutralisation of closed oxygen tanks.
- M4: supernatant and digested sludge samples, how to read the troubleshooting tables.

## What was declined and why
- Numeric ranges and limits that belong to their course or a regulator (SOUR by sludge type, air rates, SRT tables, hydrogen sulfide exposure effects, pressure figures).
- Entry, tether and cover work lists: procedures belong to the plant, and the course keeps safety to recognition and purpose.
- Rules of thumb and formulas with no textbook passage in the sources read (rate doubling per 10 C, VS converted per cu ft, TS:VS ratio).
