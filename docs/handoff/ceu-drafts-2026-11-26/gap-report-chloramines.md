## What was added beyond Royce
- Ammonia counted as nitrogen versus as NH3, with both ratios derived from atomic weights.
- Solution strength to pounds of nitrogen per gallon for aqua ammonia and ammonium sulfate, with fictional products.
- Percent reduction of a by-product group, tank water age, residual loss per day, booster ammonia and chlorine feed, pump mL/min and drum life.
- DPD interference and the free ammonia reading as a ratio check.
- Cross-references to Chlorination Principles instead of repeating free, combined and total chlorine, breakpoint and the chloramine introduction.

## Added in the gap fill (textbook sourced)
- M1: reading the breakpoint dip, CT formula, chlorine history, the 1983 EPA options, the kinds of rule behind the choice, EPA's selection factors.
- M2: bench test to find a working ratio.
- M3: test method list, target-residual balance, nitrate and blue-baby syndrome, organic matter demand, pig cleaning and looping, dechlorination of discharges.
- M4: chlorine and hypochlorite hazard recognition, safety data sheets, process safety rules, customer notification.

## Not added, and why
- Numeric ranges: operating ratio ranges, free ammonia targets, residual goals, pH and temperature ranges, tank turnover rates, storage pressures and fill limits, detection thresholds. No textbook we hold states them, and regulatory or recommended values are for the applicable rule or procedure.
- Regulatory dates, limits and rule numbers.
- Hydraulic modelling and pigging detail, equipment materials, ammonia scrubbers.
