# Gap report: Basic Safety vs Royce U `bsp` (264 slides, read in full 2026-11-17)

Covered (YES): lockout/tagout; manholes, wet wells, storm drains; trench 5 ft, shoring, sloping, trench box; trench exit ladder; spoil 2 ft from edge; hazard report and near miss; acid dilution; SDS and labels; electrical ladders, metal, rings; lifting; hazard communication and training; physical demands.

Partly covered (PART): confined-space testing (atmosphere types), ventilation and purge, attendant and harness/lifeline, toe-boards and falling objects, housekeeping and stacking, PPE set (hard hat, eyes, hearing, feet), first aid and CPR, eyewash and shower, batteries and electrolyte, electrical de-energizing, grounding, GFCI and extension cords, gas cylinders and regulators, welding and hot work, arc screens and filter lenses, painting and solvents, portable power tools, cranes and hoists, animal and insect bites, respirator fit test and medical review, fire classes and extinguishers, fire response sequence, vaccinations and hand washing, ignition sources and smoking.

Not covered (NO): SCBA and IDLH (when self-contained breathing apparatus is required); explosion-proof equipment in classified areas; hydrogen peroxide handling and first aid; cross-connection and marking of non-potable piping; food and laboratory contamination; sandblasting.

Our figures are not reused. Royce U's numeric limits (oxygen range, LEL, H2S TWA, 90 dBA, distances, fall heights, 7 working days) are NOT restated; each becomes "the applicable rule or permit sets the value".
