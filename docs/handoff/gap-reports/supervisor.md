# Required topic checklist: So You Want to be a Supervisor vs Royce U `supp` (307 slides, read in full 2026-11-17)

Teach in our own words, with our own scenarios. Do not copy Royce U wording, case stories, questions or images. Their state rules are Florida-specific (Florida Administrative Code classification tables, "licensed operator in responsible charge"). We teach the general principles, and any state-specific certification rule is written as "check your state's rules; the certifying body sets the requirement". Do NOT state Florida tables, category numbers or staffing numbers as universal rules.

## Role of a supervisor
- A supervisor is responsible for the day-to-day performance of a small group (team or shift); the shift between operator and supervisor (focus moves from doing the work to assigning, guiding, monitoring and documenting; consistency toward all staff; relationships with former peers).
- Where a supervisor sits: above operators, below a manager; what a supervisor can and cannot decide (hiring, firing and promotion power varies; discipline is usually involved); the dictionary and legal meaning of "supervisor" (who has real authority).
- The supervisor's job: guide the group toward its goals and see that every member is productive.

## Regulatory responsibility (general principles)
- The licensed operator in responsible charge carries legal responsibility for operations, decisions and staff supervision on that role; actions and failures to perform duties can have personal consequences. Teach as a concept with "check your state's rules".

## Management and organisational styles
- Autocratic, paternalistic, democratic, laissez-faire and bureaucratic: definition, what each looks like on a shift, advantages and disadvantages, when each suits a utility.
- Scenario comparisons on the same task (a filter check): how each style would handle training, checklists and accountability.
- Documentation and checklists as accountability tools; no signing a task not performed.

## Organisation charts
- Charts differ by utility; multiple layers in autocratic or paternalistic organisations; flatter in laissez-faire.

## Staffing and certification (general only)
- Categories of facility and certification class exist in most states and provinces; the supervisor must know which rule applies. Do not give tables.

## Regulatory and public safety responsibility
- Public health awareness: utility work protects communities; infection routes (waterborne and water-associated pathogens) at purpose level.
