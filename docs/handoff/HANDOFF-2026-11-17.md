## The product and the goal

Echelon Institute (repo `aybello/echelon-ai-tutor`) is exam prep for water and wastewater operators. The owner wants **the best exam prep for all of Canada and the US**, not just Ontario. Any copy that says "Ontario" where it should not is a bug the owner has already complained about several times. The owner built the original app with Manus (a separate tool that merges and deploys); this session works on branches and PRs and the owner or Manus merges.

## Why Royce U keeps coming up

RoyCEU.com is a long-running continuing-education (CE) provider for operators. The owner sees it as the incumbent to beat, and a state training regulator (PA DEP) even lists it as a recommended resource. What the owner learned, and what we confirmed by reading their free public study material:

- Their catalogue is about 35 courses. Study use is free; what they sell is **state approvals** (a list of states where their courses count toward licence renewal, for example Florida, Michigan, Ohio, Georgia, Wisconsin) and credit registration.
- Their content is old (copyright dates mostly 2003 to 2006) and thin on teaching. The Basic Hydraulics course is 148 slides but has about 10 study questions, **no explanations**, no worked area, velocity or tank examples, and some mistakes (a power example labelled water horsepower that is really brake horsepower, a wrong rounding, an unsourced rule of thumb). Breadth on pump equipment is its strength: pump types, valve types, NPSH, priming, troubleshooting.
- **Why we looked at it:** the owner asked "without extracting the slides how will you know what's correct and what we should aim to be better than". We extracted the free course text and images into a scratch folder **only to benchmark** (topic coverage, depth, errors, what learners get). We did **not** copy any of their text, questions, examples or images into the repo or product, and the owner has not asked us to. Every Echelon course is written in our own words, own scenarios and own numbers, from standard principles and official exam outlines.
- Their material is not our source of truth. Correctness comes from official outlines, standard references (EPA and state manuals), recomputed numbers and multi-model review.
- Do **not** copy, scrape into the product, or reproduce Royce U content. If you need to look at it again, their public pages are `https://www.royceu.com/public/Courses.aspx` and course slide data at `/Files/Courses/<code>/data/slide<N>.js` (`bhp` is Basic Hydraulics). `curl` works; `WebFetch` does not for this host.
- The owner's plan: offer CE credit eventually. The repo already has ten self-paced CE pilot courses (non-credit, Ontario-focused). Approval is **state by state** and nothing has been submitted anywhere. Do not claim approved CEUs or contact hours anywhere.

## How the pipelines work

**Exam bank** (`scripts/us-bank.mjs lint|build|check <bank-id>`): source files `source/conceptual.txt` (`question|correct|w1|w2|w3|explanation|level`) and `source/calculations.txt` (`question|formula|expression|unit|places|w1,w2,w3|level`) with `@Module|SOURCE|Topic` headers. Lint rules: correct answer longest in at most 35% of questions, no near-duplicate options, no banned words (always, never, only, all, every, none, cannot, completely, entirely, solely) in options, no `|` in fields. The builder adds a template sentence to calculation explanations. Review: split into halves, plant one wrong key and one altered explanation word or number per half (verify the change is real), send to two Opus agents, GPT-6.1 Sol and Gemini 4 hydrogen, apply real findings once, then stop and record leftovers in `REVIEW-RESULT.md`. `docs/handoff/tools/make_fl_review.py` and `run_fl_review.py` are the exact scripts used for Florida (edit the hard-coded scratchpad paths).

**Course drafts** live in `content/ceu-drafts/` (not live). To publish, copy the JSON to `server/ceu/courses/`, add it to `server/ceu/catalogue.ts` and `shared/ceuCatalogueData.json`, put its cover in `client/public/ceu-covers/`. PR #248 is the worked example.

**Models and keys:** the environment's proxy injects real API keys; send `Authorization: Bearer placeholder` (Google: `x-goog-api-key: placeholder` also works). Gemini OpenAI-compatible endpoint `https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/openai/chat/completions` (use `max_tokens`, 32000: Gemini spends tokens on hidden reasoning and returns empty text if the cap is small). OpenAI `https://api.openai.com/v1/chat/completions` (`max_completion_tokens`). Review models: `gpt-6.1-sol`, `gemini-4-hydrogen`; also Opus via the Agent tool. Images: OpenAI `gpt-image-2.6-flare` and `gpt-image-2.6-sunburst` via `/v1/images/generations`; Gemini images via the **native** route `/v1beta/models/gemini-3-pro-image-preview:generateContent` with `generationConfig.responseModalities ["TEXT","IMAGE"]`. Always look at generated images and say what is wrong (dial faces, equipment accuracy). Do not use generated images for anything where a precise reading matters.

## Practical tips and gotchas

- Test runner: `env -i PATH="$PATH" HOME=/tmp NODE_ENV=test PLAYWRIGHT_BROWSERS_PATH=/opt/pw-browsers node --max-old-space-size=6144 --require ./scripts/test-safety.cjs node_modules/vitest/vitest.mjs run <files>`. Type check: `node --max-old-space-size=8192 node_modules/typescript/bin/tsc --noEmit -p tsconfig.json`. About 70 tests fail in this sandbox for missing secrets and fail the same way on main.
- Worktrees are the safe way to work on a second branch (`git worktree add -b <branch> /home/user/<dir> origin/main`, symlink `node_modules`).
- Screenshots: Playwright with `NODE_PATH=.../node_modules/.pnpm/playwright@1.63.0/node_modules`, vite dev server with `VITE_OAUTH_PORTAL_URL=https://auth.example.com VITE_APP_ID=demo`, mock `/api/trpc/**`. `docs/handoff/tools/ce_shots.cjs` is an example.
- Python's PIL is broken here (use ImageMagick `convert`); Python reading untrusted files should use `-I`. `WebFetch` fails on some hosts; `curl` through the proxy works. Wikimedia rate-limits the shared proxy.
- A Stop hook asks for commits whenever the working tree is dirty; commit work-in-progress to the content branch and push.
- You cannot see the live admin dashboard or the deployed app; there is no login. Say so rather than guessing, and ask for a screenshot.
