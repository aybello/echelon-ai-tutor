# Ontario 309A Authoritative Source Facts

## Current-exam basis

Red Seal’s Construction Electrician trade page states that the current exam remains based on the **previous** Red Seal Occupational Standard while examinations based on the newer RSOS are under development. The question bank must therefore retain a versioned current-exam blueprint and must not silently switch to the new RSOS.

Source: https://red-seal.ca/eng/trades/const-elect.shtml

## Current Red Seal examination weighting

The official previous-RSOS examination weighting page specifies a 100-question interprovincial Construction Electrician examination and the following Major Work Activity distribution:

| Major Work Activity | Weight | Planned 100-question bank target |
|---|---:|---:|
| A — Performs common occupational skills | 11% | 11 |
| B — Generating, distribution and service systems | 28% | 28 |
| C — Wiring systems | 30% | 30 |
| D — Motors and control systems | 21% | 21 |
| E — Signalling and communication systems | 10% | 10 |

The page also specifies the task and sub-task taxonomy A-1 through E-28. Use these identifiers as original question blueprint objectives and question-bank metadata; do not copy proprietary source text into question explanations.

Source: https://red-seal.ca/eng/trades/constelectric/previous/exam-weightings.shtml

## Ontario scope and current curriculum transition

Skilled Trades Ontario describes 309A as Electrician — Construction and Maintenance. Its public trade page says the revised 2024 electrical-trades curriculum is phased: Level 1 from September 1, 2024; Level 2 from September 1, 2025; Levels 3 and 4 from September 1, 2026. The page describes the 309A program as 9,000 total hours, including 8,160 on-the-job hours and 840 in-school hours.

Source: https://www.skilledtradesontario.ca/trade-information/electrician-construction-and-maintenance/
