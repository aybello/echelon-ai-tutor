# 309A Flashcard Prompt and Color Audit — 2026-08-18

## Prompt refinement

The prior 200-card release displayed long assessment-style scenarios directly on the front of every card. Card fronts now use one focused scenario sentence or a short 220-character cue. The full approved answer, explanation, and takeaway remain on the revealed back of the card.

## Color refinement

The bright blue controls have been replaced with Echelon Institute deep navy (`#1E3A5F`) and electric blue (`#0047AB`) accents. Module selection, study-deck state, progress, shuffle, and the answer face use this restrained palette.

## Public verification

The public route now displays a concise front card for the 200-card deck, such as the `Motor Slip` scenario cue, alongside the deep-blue active module and study-deck controls. A live card reveal confirmed the deep navy-to-electric-blue answer face, readable white answer content, and exposed learner decision controls below it.
