# Pricing and Checkout Audit, September 16, 2026

The live pricing route at [echeloninstitute.ca/pricing](https://echeloninstitute.ca/pricing) was reviewed at desktop and mobile widths. The visual path is usable and the buyer-path choice is clear. The primary commercial risk is factual availability: the clean database currently holds 489 learner-visible OIT Water questions and 483 learner-visible OIT Wastewater questions, while the pricing source statically presents every Ontario and WPI product as available and claims 18,000+ questions.

The immediate correction is to derive the selectable and purchasable course list from verified live-bank availability. This prevents a customer from paying for a course whose learner-ready question bank has not been restored. The pricing page must state the exact verified OIT inventory, show a clear course-specific purchase option, and route unavailable courses to a no-purchase interest path rather than Stripe Checkout.

The mobile review found the core selector and buyer cards remain reachable, but the long page makes a precise first decision and immediately visible selected-course confirmation important. The next UI pass should focus on authoritative availability, a compact explanation of what the selected OIT pass includes, and a clear distinction between live purchase and unavailable-course interest registration.
