# Ontario Water Distribution and Supply naming

Ontario Class 1 to 4 courses use **Water Distribution and Supply** in their full display names. Compact interfaces use **Distribution & Supply** where space is limited.

[OWWCO's exam preparation guidance](https://owwco.ca/preparing-for-your-exam/) uses Water Distribution and Supply as the Ontario category name. This naming correction does not claim a new audit of curriculum coverage, endorsement or certification approval.

## Updated surfaces

- Homepage course cards and Ontario stream tab
- Province-aware course finder and results
- Individual pricing, purchase-gate offers and account cards
- Practice, mock-exam and flashcard headings for Classes 1 to 4
- Learner, manager and admin course names derived from the registry or exam labels
- Teams Flex course choices, Teams Annual stream selection and regional receipt names
- Ontario course landing-page metadata, server-rendered descriptions and answer-engine coverage text

## Preserved contracts

Course keys, URLs, question-bank keys, product identities, prices, CAD checkout, access duration, entitlements, study records and instructional content are unchanged. No database migration or customer-data correction is needed.

Western Canada/WPI and US course names remain **Water Distribution**. Technical guide titles and module identifiers also remain unchanged because they describe a process or stored learning topic, not the Ontario certification category.

Existing durable Stripe team products are not renamed or recreated by this change. Their billing identities and shared annual price lookup keys remain unchanged.
