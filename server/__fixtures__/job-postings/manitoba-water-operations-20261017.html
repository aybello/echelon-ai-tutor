Manitoba Transportation and Infrastructure is running a competition to fill the Water Operations Technician (EA4) position in Morris.

The Water Operations branch ensures safe and effective provincial water related infrastructure through the delivery of inspection, planning, design, maintenance, rehabilitation and construction activities. The Branch is responsible for the overall management of infrastructure inventory that includes: drains, dams, flood protection dikes, diversions, bridges, culverts and pumping stations.

Duties:
The Water Operations Technician (WOT) reports to the Superintendent and has responsibility for department staff on a project site. The WOT position consists of the following:  project coordinator, senior waterway inspector, survey crew chief, hydrometric survey technician, design, and graphics technician.

The Water Operations Technician (WOT) performs assigned moderate technical engineering related functions and is responsible for independent decisions related to hour-to-hour operations, insuring department standards, specifications, policies, procedures and sound construction and survey practices are followed, when employed in field operations.  When working as a design and graphics technician, the WOT produces preliminary construction drawings from raw survey data.
