# Exam-Bank Expansion: Sources

Grounding follows the game-content build (~/workspace/echelon/game-content/SOURCES.md and AUTHORING-BRIEF.md). Every item was authored from the source list recorded for its course below; the same lists are embedded in each course's SPEC.md and were the only sources the authoring workers were given. Sources are topic-level supporting references, not claim-level verification citations: no regulation limit or numeric standard was asserted from a source unless the value was stated in the question stem itself.

Rules applied across all courses:

- Canadian units and Canadian regulatory framing throughout.
- Ontario courses cite Ontario regulations where the topic calls for them (O. Reg. 170/03, 128/04, 129/04, 169/03).
- WPI courses do NOT cite Ontario regulations and do NOT invent provincial regulation numbers; they use generic provincial framing plus the Guidelines for Canadian Drinking Water Quality, the Wastewater Systems Effluent Regulations, and CCME material as the brief allows. Where a batch topic plan named an Ontario regulation (for example a class 2 duties topic), WPI workers covered the topic in generic provincial form with no Ontario citation.
- All scenarios are original, fictional study content. No real exam questions were copied.
