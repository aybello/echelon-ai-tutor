## Question 519 (row 45797)

Reasons: State inventory and effluent-loss boundary explicitly. Recomputed numerical result; four distinct options in matching units; removed stale repair commentary and answer-letter references.

An aeration-basin-only SRT estimate uses 6,000 m3 at 4,000 mg/L MLSS and WAS of 200 m3/day at 10,000 mg/L. Assume negligible effluent solids losses and exclude clarifier inventory. What is the estimated SRT?

A. 6.0 days

B. 9.6 days

C. 12.0 days [correct]

D. 15.0 days

Substitute the stated values with consistent units: (4000*6000)/(200*10000) = 12. Rounded to 1 decimal places, the result is 12.0 days. The Solids Retention Time (SRT) is calculated by dividing the total mass of solids in the aeration basin by the mass of solids removed daily via Waste Activated Sludge (WAS).

1. Calculate the total mass of solids in the basin:
   Mass_basin = MLSS × Basin Volume
   Mass_basin = 4,000 mg/L × 6,000 m³
   Convert units: 4,000 mg/L = 4,000 g/m³ = 4 kg/m³
   Mass_basin = 4 kg/m³ × 6,000 m³ = 24,000 kg

2. Calculate the mass of solids wasted daily:
   Mass_wasted = WAS Flow × WAS Concentration
   Mass_wasted = 200 m³/d × 10,000 mg/L
   Convert units: 10,000 mg/L = 10,000 g/m³ = 10 kg/m³
   Mass_wasted = 200 m³/d × 10 kg/m³ = 2,000 kg/d

3. Calculate the SRT:
   SRT = Mass_basin / Mass_wasted
   SRT = 24,000 kg / 2,000 kg/d = 12 days

Therefore, the SRT is 12 days.
