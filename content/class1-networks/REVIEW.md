## Water Distribution

### 2001 — Network hydraulics

Two closed service taps share one pressure zone. Which location normally has lower static pressure?

- **A.** The tap with a shorter service
- **B.** The tap at higher elevation
- **C.** The tap with a newer meter
- **D.** The tap nearest a large main

**Answer: B. The tap at higher elevation**

With the same hydraulic grade, greater elevation leaves less pressure head.

Supporting topic reference: [US EPA — EPANET hydraulic modelling](https://www.epa.gov/water-research/epanet).
