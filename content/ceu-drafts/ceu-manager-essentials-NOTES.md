# Manager Essentials: draft notes

Status: draft, files only. Not registered in any catalogue, not live, not reviewed by GPT-6.2 Sol, GPT-6.2 Astra or Gemini hydrogen. Opus review not run.

## Structure (checked with a script)
- 4 modules: responsibilities and compliance; finance, rates and budget requests; utilising people; planning, risk and decisions.
- 6 quick checks per module (24), 20 final questions, 20 alternate final questions. Every question has four choices and a valid correct index.
- Unique question ids and prompts. Correct answers are the longest option in 0 of 64 questions. Answer positions are balanced (5 of 20 in each position for the finals and for the alternates).
- No banned absolute words in any option (always, never, only, all, every, none, cannot, completely, entirely, solely).
- Activities total 200 minutes, matching plannedMinutes. The last activity of the last module names the final assessment.
- Lessons: 1,025 to 1,359 words, with 8 or 9 level-three headings each. Module 4 is near the low end of the range.
- Diagrams: three original inline SVG figures (modules 1, 2 and 4). The draft rule asks for at least five; this is a shortfall.
- Cover photo: one illustrative fictional photo, to be added once generated.

## Constants and arithmetic used (recomputed by hand and by script)
- 2,080 hours per full-time equivalent (illustrative planning constant, not a rule).
- Staffing: 3 shifts x 365 days x 8 hours = 8,760 hours; 8,760 / 2,080 = 4.21 FTE. Lab example: 8 x 260 = 2,080 h = 1.00 FTE. Two-operator example: 730 x 12 h = 8,760 h = 4.21 FTE.
- Revenue: 1,200 accounts x $30 x 12 = $432,000; 45,000,000 gal / 1,000 x $4.10 = $184,500; total $616,500; fixed share 70 percent. Conservation 10 percent of volume revenue = $18,450.
- Other fictional examples: 800 accounts x $35 x 12 = $336,000; 30,000 x $3.80 = $114,000; total $450,000; fixed share 74.7 percent.
- Payback: $240,000 / $36,000 = 6.7 years; $180,000 / $28,000 = 6.4 years; $150,000 / $20,000 = 7.5 years; $120,000 / $15,000 = 8.0 years; $200,000 / $25,000 = 8.0 years.
- Chemicals: 4,800 lb x $0.90 = $4,320 per month, $51,840 per year; 3,200 lb x $1.25 = $4,000 per month, $48,000 per year.
- Turnover: 3 / 12 = 25 percent; 2 / 15 = 13.3 percent; 5 / 25 = 20 percent; 6 / 40 = 15 percent. Replacement at an illustrative $8,000 each gives $24,000.
- Risk score: 4 x 3 = 12 versus 2 x 5 = 10; 5 x 2 = 10 versus 3 x 4 = 12.
- Cost range: $200,000 +/- 10 percent = $180,000 to $220,000.
- Worked example: senior operator 56 h/week to a target of 45 h = 11 h to move; 4 + 4 + 3 = 11 h.
