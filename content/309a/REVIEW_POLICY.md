# Echelon 309A research-review policy

The 309A question bank is reviewed internally by Ay, Codex, Claude, and Manus. An outside subject-matter expert is not a release requirement.

## Required review method

1. Map every question to the current official Red Seal Major Work Activity, task, and subtask taxonomy.
2. Check factual claims against the official sources recorded in `current-exam-source-manifest.json` and record source/version drift before approval.
3. Independently recompute every calculation and confirm that the keyed answer appears in the worked steps.
4. Check each stem for one defensible best answer, plausible distractors, unsupported assumptions, ambiguity, answer-length cues, and duplicate wording.
5. Use a second Echelon reviewer for technical approval and beta release. The author may perform editorial review but may not be the sole technical or release reviewer.
6. Resolve disagreements by returning to the official source and documenting the decision. Ay makes the final product-release decision.

## Source boundary

The current 500-question bank uses public official blueprint and occupational-standard material and original Echelon wording. Canadian Electrical Code rule/table questions remain outside this bank until Echelon has legitimate access to the applicable edition. This is a source-rights boundary, not an outside-review requirement.

## Release record

The review ledger must identify the author, the second Echelon reviewer, review type, decision, date, source set, blueprint version, and immutable content hash. Public beta content must also retain the existing emergency-unpublish path and must not claim to be an official Red Seal or Skilled Trades Ontario exam.
