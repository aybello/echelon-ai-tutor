# Review result: Pennsylvania wastewater collection (Class E, Subclass 4) bank

Reviewed 2026-11-16 by Opus reviewers (Claude models), the owner's verification authority for this content.

## What was done

All 250 questions were read in two chunks of 125. Each chunk also held two deliberately planted errors, to show that the reviewer was reading. The reviewers checked each key, whether a wrong option could also be right, each explanation, each calculation redone from the question's own constants, and the dossier's scope exclusions (no numeric thresholds, no procedures, no emergency steps, no laboratory content).

## Result

- **Planted errors caught: 4 of 4.**
- **Real problems: 2 of 250 (0.8%).** Both corrected:
  - PA-E4-COLL-044, jetting: the key was right, but the explanation said the nozzle is pulled upstream. The rear-facing jets drive it upstream and the hose reel pulls it back. Explanation and one option reworded.
  - PA-E4-COLL-222, percent solids: a lab total-solids calculation, which the dossier excludes as laboratory content. Replaced with the same percent arithmetic in a collection setting (manholes showing inflow).
- Both fixes are applied to the source and the package is rebuilt (250 questions, answers balanced across positions, no answer-cue flags).

## What this review does not cover

- The reviewers made no web checks. They judged from knowledge and redid every calculation. The writers' flagged questions (WRITER-FLAGS.md) were reviewed like any other, and none was marked wrong.
- The Pennsylvania DEP collection modules the exam is built on could not be reached from the review environment, so the questions are not checked against them line by line.
- No question here has been imported, activated or shown to learners.
