@Trickling Filters|TCEQWW|Filter media and distribution

Why does an activated sludge tank need enough oxygen?|Aerobic microorganisms use oxygen to break down the organic load in the mixed liquor.|Oxygen raises the density of the mixed liquor so the solids settle faster.|A higher dissolved gas concentration keeps the floc suspended in the tank.|It strips dissolved contaminants from the liquid before they reach the clarifier.|The biology needs dissolved oxygen to oxidize BOD.
