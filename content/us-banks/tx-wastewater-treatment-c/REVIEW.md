# Texas Wastewater Treatment Plant Operator, Class C (TCEQ), need-to-know practice bank (Narrow): 402-question review

402 original practice questions: 284 conceptual and 118 calculations, in US customary units. Scope is Narrow. The questions are aligned to the 20 TCEQ wastewater treatment need-to-know categories for the Class C level. They are practice questions aligned to the TCEQ need-to-know categories; not the official exam blueprint. No weights or counts are claimed. Calculations state every assumption; hypothetical values in stems are not TCEQ limits. Class A and B, collection classes, and water classes are excluded.

### 1: Operator duties and records (medium)

A chlorine residual was below the permit minimum but was not written down during a busy shift. What should the operator do?

- **A.** Enter the reading taken at the start of the shift, since the plant was in range then.
- **B.** Write the entry from the shift notes at the end of the week, once the crew has time.
- **C.** Average the readings from the two shifts on either side so the log stays in the normal range.
- **D.** Record the reading as measured, with its time, and report it to the supervisor.

**Answer: D. Record the reading as measured, with its time, and report it to the supervisor.**

Records must show what was measured and when. Substituting or estimating values hides a compliance problem and can be a violation.

### 2: Operator duties and records (easy)

The permit is the working standard for daily operation. Keeping it at hand lets operators compare results and conditions as they happen.
