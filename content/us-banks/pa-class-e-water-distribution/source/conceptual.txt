What is nitrification in a chloraminated system?|Microbial conversion of ammonia to nitrite and nitrate|The mixing of chlorine and ammonia at the feed point|The removal of nitrate by a filter at the plant|The corrosion of iron pipes by water with high nitrate|Nitrifying bacteria use ammonia as an energy source and convert it through nitrite to nitrate, which can use up the chloramine residual.|e
