# Pennsylvania Wastewater Treatment: Part I General Examination component only: 122-question review

122 original practice questions for the Part I General Examination component of the Pennsylvania wastewater treatment route: 93 conceptual and 29 calculations, in US customary units. Scope follows the narrowed dossier (pa-wastewater-treatment, Narrow). Passing a Part I practice set does not certify anyone and does not cover any Part II examination.

Correct answers are shown beneath each item. See README.md for scope, sources and limits.

### 1: Purpose and stages of wastewater treatment (easy)

What is the main purpose of treating wastewater before it is discharged?

- **A.** To add nutrients so the receiving stream can support a larger population of fish and plants
- **B.** To reduce pollutants so the receiving water and public health are protected
- **C.** To make the effluent safe enough to drink directly from a household tap without further handling
- **D.** To increase the volume of clean water returned to the river so downstream users have more supply

**Answer: B. To reduce pollutants so the receiving water and public health are protected**

Treatment removes or reduces contaminants so that the discharge does not harm the stream or the people who use it.
