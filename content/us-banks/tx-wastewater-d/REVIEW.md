### 1: TCEQ authority over discharges (easy)

What is the main purpose of a TCEQ waste discharge permit for a domestic treatment plant?

- **A.** It sets the rates the utility may charge customers for sewer service
- **B.** It sets the conditions for releasing treated wastewater to state waters
- **C.** It lists the continuing education hours each operator must complete yearly
- **D.** It transfers ownership of the treatment works from the city to the state

**Answer: B. It sets the conditions for releasing treated wastewater to state waters**

A discharge permit states what a facility may release and how it must monitor and report that discharge. It protects the receiving water and does not set rates or ownership.

### 2: TCEQ authority over discharges (easy)

Why does the state give a commission authority over discharges to waters of the state?

- **B.** To set the salaries that cities must pay to treatment plant staff
- **C.** To decide which contractor builds each new interceptor in a city
- **D.** To protect surface water and groundwater from pollution by wastewater

**Answer: D. To protect surface water and groundwater from pollution by wastewater**

Waste discharge rules exist to protect the waters that receive treated effluent and the people who use them. Contracting and salary matters are outside that purpose.
