# Review result: California Collection System Maintenance Grade 1 (CWEA) bank

Reviewed 2026-11-16 by Opus reviewers (Claude models), the owner's verification authority for this content.

## What was done

All 250 questions were read in two chunks of 125. Each chunk also held two deliberately planted errors (one wrong answer key and one false statement or wrong number in an explanation), to show the reviewer was reading. The reviewers checked each key, whether a wrong option could also be right, each explanation, each calculation redone from the question's own constants, and the dossier's exclusions (entry level only, no regulatory thresholds or timeframes, no step-by-step safety procedures, no higher-grade or treatment-plant content). After fixes, the 33 changed questions were reviewed a second time.

## Result

- **Planted errors caught: 4 of 4.**
- **Real problems found and corrected:**
  - **Giveaway wording, about 32 questions (Tools and equipment, Mapping).** Wrong options had been padded with filler phrases to pass the length check, so the correct answer was the only clean, specific option. All wrong options were rewritten without filler, and a few distractors were lengthened with real content so the correct answer is no longer the longest.
  - **CA-CSM1-195, ambiguous stem.** The question did not say why the crew was at the manhole. The stem now says scheduled maintenance, and the key was reworded so it does not repeat the stem's words.
  - **Second-pass fixes (from the recheck of the 33 changed questions):** CA-CSM1-080 had two near-duplicate wrong options; 100 had a wrong option an operator could defend; 103 and 084 had awkward wording; 094 and 087 had small wording issues. All fixed.
- All 40 calculations were redone by the reviewers; every keyed answer matched.
- The package was rebuilt after the fixes: 250 questions (210 conceptual, 40 calculations), answers balanced across positions, build check passes.

## What this review does not cover

- The CWEA candidate handbook was not available to the writers or reviewers here. The questions follow the exam outline and domain weights in the dossier and general, well-established collection-system practice. They are not checked line by line against the handbook.
- Weaker wrong options remain in a few questions (for example CA-CSM1-101). They are not wrong, only easy to rule out.
- No question here has been imported, activated or shown to learners.
